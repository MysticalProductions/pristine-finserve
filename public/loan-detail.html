FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum CIBIL score required for a home loan? +

Most banks prefer a CIBIL score of 750 or above. However, we work with multiple lenders who may consider scores between 650-750 with slightly different terms.

Can I transfer my existing home loan to get a lower rate? +

Yes, you can transfer your home loan balance to another bank offering lower interest rates. We can help you with the entire balance transfer process.

What documents are required for a home loan? +

Basic documents include identity proof (Aadhaar/PAN), address proof, income proof (salary slips/IT returns), bank statements for 6 months, and property documents.

How long does home loan approval take? +

In-principle approval can be obtained within 24-48 hours. Final approval and disbursal typically takes 7-14 working days after complete documentation.

What is the maximum home loan I can get? +

You can get up to ₹10 Crores depending on your income, property value, and repayment capacity. We offer up to 90% of the property value as loan.
